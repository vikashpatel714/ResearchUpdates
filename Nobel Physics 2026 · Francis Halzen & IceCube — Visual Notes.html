Step 1 · Neutrino kya hota hai?

Neutrino ek bahut chhota, bahut halka particle hai. Na charge, na takrane ki aadat. Isliye wo deewar, pahaad, poori Dharti ke aar-paar nikal jaata hai. Har second lagbhag 100 trillion neutrinos (zyadatar Sooraj se) tumhari body se guzar jaate hain — tumhe pata bhi nahi chalta.

🟡 Roshni: 0 deewar par ruki🔵 Neutrino: 0 aar-paar nikle

Seekh: Neutrino = hawa jo deewar ke aar-paar. Isi wajah se pakadna bahut mushkil hai.

Step 2 · IceCube ka jugaad

Neutrino ko seedha dekh nahi sakte. Isliye South Pole ki 1 km³ barf ke andar hazaron sensors dabaye gaye. Kabhi-kabhi ek neutrino barf ke atom se takra jaata hai aur ek muon banata hai. Ye muon barf me roshni se tez chalta hai, to neela Cherenkov prakash nikalta hai (roshni ka “sonic boom”).

Step 3 · Neutrino aate kahan se hain?

Brahmand ke sabse hinsak jagah: black hole ke paas wali galaxies, supernova, blazars. Wahan se neutrino seedha nikalta hai, Dharti ko cheerta hai aur barf me pakda jaata hai.

Step 4 · Ye “nayi khagol vigyan” kyun?

Pehle hum brahmand sirf roshni se dekhte the. Neutrino ek naya khidki hai. Dekho baaki raaste kyun kamzor hain:

📖 Mini glossary

Neutrino: bahut halka, bina charge ka particle.
Muon: electron ka bhaari bhai; neutrino takrane par banta hai.
Cherenkov light: medium me roshni se tez particle ka neela chamak.
DOM: IceCube ka roshni-sensor module.
Blazar: black hole wali galaxy jo apna jet hamari taraf phenkti hai.
Astrophysical: brahmand ke baahar se aaya (Dharti ke vayumandal se nahi).
